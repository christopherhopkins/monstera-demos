Monstera Live demos

Try Monstera as the person who would use it.

Member and client portals for WordPress, one plugin, three kinds of program. Pick a point of view below. The demo opens in your browser, already signed in as that person, with a note at the top saying who you are and what to try.

What you get
A throwaway WordPress site running in your browser on WordPress Playground, with Monstera installed and a program seeded for that use case.
What it costs you
About a minute on the first boot. Nothing is saved when you close the tab, and no email ever leaves it.
Signing in
Done for you. Every person in a demo uses the password password, should you want to switch.
